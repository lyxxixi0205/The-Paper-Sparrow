# The Paper Sparrow

**The Paper Sparrow** is an online literary news website dedicated to books, reading, and everything in between. Inspired by traditional news platforms, the website brings together literature, entertainment, and digital media in a creative and engaging format.

## About

The Paper Sparrow was created as a space for readers to explore book-related content, from literary discussions to entertainment inspired by popular novels and series.

The website currently features a homepage and a collection of memes inspired by Sarah J. Maas's *Throne of Glass* series.

## Website Content

- **Homepage:** The main landing page of The Paper Sparrow.
- **Throne of Glass Memes:** A collection of memes inspired by the *Throne of Glass* book series. :D

## Technologies Used

- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript

## Purpose

The Paper Sparrow aims to bring literature and entertainment together through a digital news-inspired platform, making book-related content fun, accessible, and enjoyable for readers.
